PageMate

Reads pages aloud with voices from your own Kokoro server. Page text goes to that server and nowhere else.

1. Your Kokoro server

The IP address and port of your Kokoro server. /v1 is added automatically. When asked, allow the extension to access this address (and your local network, if the browser asks).

2. Voice and speed

Voices starting with a are American English, b British; f/m = female/male. af_heart is a good start.

1.0×

3. Reading

Handy for courses and multi-page articles: click “Next” and reading carries on with the new page. Turn off to stop reading whenever you leave a page.

4. Saving pages and videos

Save the page you’re on as a PDF, Word or Markdown file (with or without its images), or download a video from it for your own archive. Open it from the right-click menu on the toolbar icon, the right-click menu on a page or video, the buttons in the player, or Alt+Shift+S / Alt+Shift+V.

Needed once: access to the sites you save from (to read the page and fetch its images and videos) and to your Downloads folder. The extension still only touches a page when you ask it to.

When on (and access is allowed), the extension remembers the addresses of video files and streams each tab loads — in memory, for this browser session only — so the Download a video list can show them. Nothing is sent anywhere. Videos protected by DRM can’t be saved.

5. Notes

Highlight text on any page, add notes and tags, and export them as Markdown. Your notes stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded. Select text and right-click → Highlight selection (or Highlight and add a note…), or right-click the toolbar icon → Notes for this page. Set your own keyboard shortcuts for Open / close the Notes sidebar and Highlight the selected text at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Needs the access allowed in “4. Saving pages and videos”. The extension only checks, on each page you open, whether you have notes for that exact page address; pages without notes are left alone.

Also in Notes: citations, flashcards for Anki, video notes, Word/PDF export, and a sealed Capture (evidence) package on the Save page. The first sealed capture asks for one extra permission (“capture pages”).

How to use it

  • Click the extension icon, or press Alt+Shift+R, to read the page from where you are. Press again to stop.
  • Alt+Shift+P pauses and resumes.
  • Hold Alt and click any paragraph to jump there, or press the ⌖ button in the player and then click a paragraph (useful if your desktop uses Alt+click to move windows).
  • With text selected, the icon or Alt+Shift+R reads just the selection.
  • Select text, right-click, and choose Read selection aloud to read just that part.
  • Shortcuts can be changed at brave://extensions/shortcuts (or chrome://extensions/shortcuts).
  • Right-click the toolbar icon for Save page… and Download a video….
  • Pin the icon: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to PageMate.
