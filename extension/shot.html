Keep it

Redactions and pixelation are burned into saved and copied images. The original is only kept if you seal it as evidence (below).

Share

Social sites can’t receive a picture from a link. The buttons copy the picture to your clipboard, then open that site’s post window with the page link filled in: paste the image into the post (Ctrl+V). Check what the picture shows before you post it.

Seal as evidence

The sealed package holds the untouched screenshot, a version with your marks if you made any, the page address, a SHA-256 manifest and a chain-of-custody record. The time is your computer’s clock unless you add a trusted timestamp.
