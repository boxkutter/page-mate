What you get

One .zip containing:

  • capture.mhtml — the page exactly as this browser has it now, in a single file that opens in any Chromium browser
  • page-text.txt — the readable text of the page
  • notes.md / notes.json — your highlights and notes, if you have any
  • screenshot.png, page-details.json, headers.json, entities.csv, trail.csv — what the page looked like, how it was served, what it contains, and what you looked at, when you choose them
  • manifest.sig, timestamp.tsr — optional signature and trusted timestamp, checkable with openssl
  • manifest.json, SHA256SUMS and chain-of-custody.md — when it was captured (UTC), by whom, the address, the browser, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of every file. Anyone can check the package is unchanged with sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS

Honest limits: unless you add a trusted timestamp, the time comes from this computer’s clock and is not verified by an outside authority; unless you sign it, the files are fingerprinted, not digitally signed. Keep the manifest fingerprint shown after capture somewhere independent (email it to yourself, a case system, a notary). Content that loads later or differs per visitor may not be in a snapshot.
